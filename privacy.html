Privacy Policy

GOMEME · Last updated: July 28, 2026 · Contact: hello@gomeme.app

This Privacy Policy describes how GOMEME (“we”, “the App”) handles information when you use the GOMEME iOS application. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

1. What we collect

  • Location — used only to show nearby meme spawns on the map and related gameplay. Location is processed on-device when possible; we do not sell location data.
  • Motion / HealthKit (steps) — optional, used to count walking/workout progress more accurately than GPS alone.
  • Camera / microphone — used only for AR catch features when you open them.
  • Account data — display name, optional email, or a local wallet key identifier. We do not store your 12-word recovery phrase on our servers.
  • Gameplay progress — cards, balance, quests and settings stored primarily on your device (local storage).
  • Diagnostics — standard crash/performance signals from Apple / Expo may be processed to keep the App stable.

2. How we use data

  • Provide core gameplay (map, catch, collection, profile).
  • Remember your session and preferences on this device.
  • Improve reliability and fix bugs.
  • Comply with App Store and legal requirements.

3. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We may use infrastructure providers (hosting, analytics, push) solely to operate the App. Data may be disclosed if required by law.

4. Retention & deletion

Most gameplay data lives on your device. You can delete your account anytime in the App: Profile → Settings → Delete account. This clears local account and progress data on the device. If server-side accounts are enabled later, deletion will also remove associated cloud records within a reasonable period (typically ≤30 days), except where law requires retention.

5. Children

GOMEME is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). Do not use the App if you are under that age.

6. Your choices

  • Deny or revoke Location / Motion / Camera permissions in iOS Settings.
  • Sign out or Delete account from in-app Settings.
  • Contact us to ask about your data: hello@gomeme.app.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date above will change. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
